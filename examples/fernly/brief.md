# Video ad brief · Fernly · Plant reviews

> Stage 1 output. Fictional brand, portfolio pilot. Chosen over Stillwave and Driftpay on 2026-09-26,
> on one condition: it must pass the go/no-go test (`test-plan.md`) before the full budget is spent.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Brand / product | Fernly, a plant-care app that tells you when and how to water each plant |
| Campaign | Plant reviews (launch) |
| Platforms | TikTok first · Reels/Stories · Shorts · Meta feed |
| Length | 15s master |
| Statics | 2 review cards (five-star plant, one-star plant) |
| Style mode | ugc, from the plant's point of view, no people |
| Voice | Arthur (Higgsfield preset), deadpan critic |

## 1. The one thing
Fernly tells you what your plant needs, so it stops dying.

## 2. Audience
24 to 38, renters with 3 or more houseplants, half of them quietly dying. They know the guilt. They scroll past tips; they stop for a joke they recognise.

## 3. Insight
People kill plants by caring too much at the wrong time, not by neglect alone. The plant has been trying to tell them.

## 4. Hook angles (3 kept)
| # | Line | First frame, sound off |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "My human is trying to drown me." | Water pouring straight at the camera from a watering can |
| 2 | "One star. Would not live here again." | The same, with a one-star caption |
| 3 | "Day 3 of being overwatered." | Wet soil macro, a puddle sitting on top |

## 5. Mandatories
No faces or hands on screen. The app screen is real UI, added in the edit. Captions on every spoken line. AI-generated content label where the platform requires it.

## 6. Success metric
Hook rate, and shares (humour is judged by shares, not only views).
