## 1. The one thing
Money from a client abroad arrives as fast as from next door.

## 2. Audience
Freelancers and small studios, 25 to 45, who bill clients in other countries and wait days for the money, with fees they can't predict.

## 3. Insight
An invoice sent abroad feels like it disappears. The ad makes the trip visible and short.

## 4. Hook angles (3 kept)
| # | Line | First frame, sound off |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Your invoice is leaving." | A paper invoice on a paper desk, one corner already lifting |
| 2 | "This invoice gets paid tomorrow." | The same frame |
| 3 | "Watch an invoice cross the world." | The plane over the paper map |

## 5. Mandatories
No figures, dates or brand names generated on paper: all type is set in the edit. "1 day" and "free account" are claims and go to legal before any real use. AI-generated content label where the platform requires it.

## 6. Success metric
LinkedIn: click-through and dwell. Meta: hook rate.
